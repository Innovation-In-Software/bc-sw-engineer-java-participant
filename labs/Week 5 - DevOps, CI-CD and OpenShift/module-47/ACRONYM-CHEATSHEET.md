## Professional skills

| Term | Full form | Simple meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **ADR** | Architecture Decision Record | Short note explaining an architecture choice and why. |
| **Agile** | — | Iterative delivery with frequent feedback. |
| **Kanban** | — | Visual workflow board limiting work in progress. |
| **KPI** | Key Performance Indicator | Metric that shows progress toward a business goal. |
| **OKR** | Objectives and Key Results | Goal-setting framework: objective + measurable results. |
| **Retrospective** | — | Team review of what went well and what to improve. |
| **RFC** | Request for Comments | Proposal document for feedback before deciding. |
| **Scrum** | — | Agile framework with sprints, standups, and roles. |
| **SMART** | Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound | Checklist for writing clear goals. |
| **Stakeholder** | — | Person/group affected by or interested in the project. |
